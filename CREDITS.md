# Credits and sources

## Project assets

Wildhaven’s game logic, interface, procedural environments, ranger, animal meshes, generated materials, and synthesized sound are created for this project. The visual style uses natural colours and proportional, stylized anatomy. No external image, model, font, or audio download is required while playing.

## Conservation references

The species descriptions, threat summaries, and conservation categories used in the field journal are based on the sources listed in `src/missions.js`. These links are for reference and are not required to play.

| Species | References |
| --- | --- |
| Tiger (*Panthera tigris*) | [Smithsonian’s National Zoo: Tiger](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/tiger) |
| Asian elephant (*Elephas maximus*) | [U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service: Asian elephant](https://www.fws.gov/cites/species/asian-elephant) |
| Sunda pangolin (*Manis javanica*) | [Singapore NParks: Sunda pangolin](https://biodiversitysg.nparks.gov.sg/our-biodiversity/mammals/other-mammals/sunda-pangolin/); [IUCN Red List assessment](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2019-3.RLTS.T12763A123584856.en) |
| Hawksbill turtle (*Eretmochelys imbricata*) | [Singapore NParks: Hawksbill turtle](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/florafaunaweb/fauna/2/6/262); [IUCN Marine Turtle Specialist Group: Conservation statuses](https://www.iucn-mtsg.org/statuses) |

The reserve, individual animals, rescue situations, puzzles, and veterinary interactions are fictional. Animal care is simplified for gameplay and is performed by trained teams in the story. The game is not affiliated with the organisations linked above.
